Privacy

Effective 6 September 2026. Receipt Packet stores receipt records in your browser.

Your receipt records

Receipt images, amounts, dates, categories, and notes are encrypted in your browser using your passphrase. Receipt Packet does not upload, inspect, sell, or sync these records. Exports are made on your device and go only where you save or send them.

Licenses

If you restore a supporter license, its token is kept in browser storage. The app may send it to this product’s same-origin verification endpoint. That endpoint forwards the token to the Sociobot billing API and uses network address information briefly to limit request bursts. License responses are not stored in the offline cache.

Browser storage

Clearing site data removes this device’s receipt records. Keep encrypted backups. Uninstalling the app may also remove browser storage. This product has no analytics or advertising tracker.

Privacy requests

Email privacy@sociobot.in.
