Privacy

Effective 28 August 2026. Receipt Packet is local-first by design.

Your receipt data

Receipt images, amounts, dates, categories, and notes are encrypted in your browser’s IndexedDB using your passphrase. The passphrase is not stored. We do not upload, inspect, sell, or sync this data. Exports are created on your device and go only where you save or send them.

Licensing

If you buy or verify a supporter license, the token is stored in localStorage. At most once per day, it is sent to this product’s same-origin verification endpoint, which forwards it to the Sociobot billing API. The endpoint processes network address information transiently to enforce a burst limit and does not place license responses in offline caches. Sociobot/Dodo processes checkout as merchant of record; their checkout privacy terms apply.

Device storage

Clearing site data removes the vault. Keep encrypted backups. Uninstalling the PWA may also remove data depending on your browser. There is no analytics or advertising tracker in this application.
